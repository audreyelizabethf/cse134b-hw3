How it's made

  1. Wedging: knead the clay to remove air bubbles.
  2. Throwing: centering and shaping the clay on the wheel.
  3. Trimming: carving the foot once the piece is leather-hard.
  4. Bisque firing: the first trip through the kiln.
  5. Glazing: dipping or brushing on color.
  6. Glaze firing: the final firing. Makes the pieces food-safe.
